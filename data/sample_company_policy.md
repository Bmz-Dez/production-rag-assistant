\# Acme Corp – Internal Knowledge Base (Sample)



\## Data Retention Policy



Acme Corp retains customer data for a maximum of 24 months after the end of the contractual relationship, unless a longer period is required by law (e.g. tax or accounting regulations in the EU).



After the retention period, data is securely deleted using industry-standard wiping procedures.



Employees may request access to their own data at any time by contacting privacy@acme.example.



\## Remote Work Policy



Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. 

Core collaboration hours are 10:00–15:00 CET.

All company devices must use full-disk encryption and the corporate VPN when accessing internal systems.



\## AI Usage Guidelines



Employees are encouraged to use approved generative AI tools for productivity.

Confidential customer data, source code, and unpublished product plans must never be pasted into public LLM interfaces.

Only the company-approved internal AI assistant may process sensitive information.

All AI-generated code must be reviewed by a human before merging.



\## Expense Policy



Travel expenses above 500 EUR require pre-approval from a director.

Receipts must be submitted within 14 days via the expense portal.

Team dinners are capped at 50 EUR per person.
